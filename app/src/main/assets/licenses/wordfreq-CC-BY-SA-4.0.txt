KeyLingo word-frequency data
============================

Which files: these two bundled data files contain material adapted from
wordfreq 3.1.1 by Robyn Speer (https://github.com/rspeer/wordfreq), "large"
word lists (wordfreq: Copyright 2022 Robyn Speer):

  * assets/lexicon/es_words.tsv  -- the whole file (Spanish word list and ranks)
  * assets/lexicon/en_words.tsv  -- the rank column (the English words themselves
    come from SCOWL, under SCOWL's own permissive terms)

Changes made by KeyLingo (as required by Section 3(a)(1)(B)):
  * words filtered to alphabetic tokens; frequency values converted to rank order;
  * Spanish: spellings validated against the RLA-ES Hunspell dictionary;
    profanity marked "never suggest";
  * English: ranks merged with KeyLingo's own curated conversational ranks
    (the better of the two is kept); lowercase forms of personal names not
    promoted; crude words and slurs marked "never suggest";
  * rows re-ordered by the keyboard's match key (no change to the ranks).
Tools: tools/build_spanish_lexicon.py, tools/rerank_english_by_frequency.py,
tools/sort_lexicon_by_key.py in the KeyLingo source.

- Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia (<http://www.wikipedia.org>)

It contains data from various SUBTLEX word lists: SUBTLEX-US, SUBTLEX-UK,
SUBTLEX-CH, SUBTLEX-DE, and SUBTLEX-NL, created by Marc Brysbaert et al.
(see citations below) and available at
<http://crr.ugent.be/programs-data/subtitle-frequencies>.
